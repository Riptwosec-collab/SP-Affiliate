# SP-Affiliate 2.7.1 — Pearl typography and motion

The light editorial theme now uses the embedded Noto Sans Thai consistently, with stronger headings, clearer labels, higher-contrast secondary text and tabular numeric figures. White pearl surfaces blend into pale blue and lavender, with subtle borders and layered shadows.

Product cards have a restrained pointer-driven 3D tilt and highlight on fine-pointer devices. Touch devices keep a small button press response. Sections translate gently into view without ever starting hidden. A thin header indicator reflects page scroll progress; it does not represent business metrics.

Motion respects the system reduced-motion preference, the existing app setting and flat-surface preference. Animations and pointer frames are cancelled on preference changes or page hiding. The body observer ignores unrelated scroll classes so scrolling does not repeatedly restart entrances. No animation library, external font, data migration or service connection is added.

Validation: storefront 16 and upgrade 15 checks (31 total), including keyboard rail access, motion preference changes, unchanged workspace data, TH/EN at 320–1920px, draft recovery, Undo/Redo, revisions, import/merge and standalone offline HTML. Physical iPhone/Safari remains untested.
